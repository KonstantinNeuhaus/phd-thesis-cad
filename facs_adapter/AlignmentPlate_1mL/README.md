\# FACS Alignment Plate



A laser-cut acrylic calibration plate for aligning the sort position of a FACS instrument with the centre of the 1 mL Petri dishes held in the FACS adapter in this repository. The plate mounts directly onto the FACS adapter in place of the Petri dishes and features engraved concentric rings at each Petri dish position to make sort drop landing positions visible during calibration.

\## Fabrication



\### Method

CO₂ laser cutter (Time Technology tt-900, 120W Laser)



\### Material

\*\*Clear acrylic sheet, 4 mm thickness\*\*



\### Laser Settings

| Operation | Power | Speed | 

|-----------|-----------|--------------------------|

| Cut (outer profile) and center ring | 65% | 15 mm/s |

| Engrave (concentric rings) | 30% | 500 mm/s |
