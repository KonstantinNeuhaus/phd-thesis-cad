# Petri Dish Parameter Screen

A multi-well screening plate designed to identify the optimal agar depth and dish diameter for *E. coli* colony biofilm growth. The plate contains holes of varying depths and diameters arranged in a single part that fits inside a standard 90 mm Petri dish for incubation.

This part was used to determine that a dish diameter of 16 mm and depth of 5 mm (1 mL agar volume) supports normal colony growth while minimising reagent consumption.

---
## Files

For each design the Autodesk Inventor 2026 .ipt file, a .step file, a .stl as well as the .3mf print project is provided.

---
## Design Specifications

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Hole depth | 4–10 mm |
| Hole diameter | 10–20 mm |
| Resulting agar volumes | ~300–3100 µL |
| Outer dimensions | Fits inside a 90 mm Petri dish |

---

## Fabrication

### Printer
Ultimaker S5 FDM printer

### Material
**Polypropylene (PP) filament** (Ultimaker)

### Print Settings
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Layer height | 0.15 mm |
| Wall thickness | 0.8 mm |
| Top/bottom thickness | 1.2 mm |
| Infill density | 45% |
> **Note:** PP filament requires careful bed adhesion management. I printed on a glass bed coated with Magigoo's PP adhesive pen.
---

## Post-Processing

Sterilise by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 minutes before use. No other post-processing is required.
