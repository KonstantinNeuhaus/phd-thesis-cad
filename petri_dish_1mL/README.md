\## Post-Processing Protocol



Post-processing is critical for biocompatibility. I followed the following steps.

\*\*1. IPA wash\*\*

Wash printed parts in >99.5% isopropanol with manual agitation for 20 minutes to remove uncured resin.



\*\*2. Drying\*\*

Dry parts with compressed nitrogen.



\*\*3. Post-curing\*\*

Post-cure for 60 minutes at 60°C in a Formlabs Form Cure unit.



\*\*4. Second IPA soak\*\*

Submerge cured parts in >99.5% isopropanol for 24 hours to ensure no traces of uncured resin remain on the parts.



\*\*5. Water wash\*\*

Thoroughly wash parts with ultrapure water and submerge in ultrapure water for an additional 24 hours to remove all traces of isopropanol.



\*\*6. Sterilisation\*\*

Autoclave at 121°C for 15 minutes before use.
