# Microscope Stage Insert

A custom stage insert for imaging *E. coli* colony biofilms grown in 1 mL Petri dishes on an inverted confocal microscope. The insert is designed to hold the Petri dish in an inverted orientation so that colonies can be imaged through the optically clear base of the dish, while preventing agar dehydration during imaging.
The insert was designed for use with a Nikon Ti-E inverted microscope body equipped with an ASI MS-2000 motorised stage, but the design can be adapted for other stage formats.

## Design Features

**Humidity control**
The space between the objective and the Petri dish is sealed with cling film to prevent agar evaporation during imaging. Agar dehydration causes significant axial focus drift over time, making long z-stack acquisitions unreliable.

**Cling film mounting system**
Two rings sandwich the cling film and are held in place by a screw at each corner. The rings attach to the main stage insert body via four embedded magnets, allowing quick removal and reattachment between experiments. On the objective side, the cling film is fixed with the heating element of the objective heater.

**Magnetic attachment**
Four magnet positions are machined into the main body. The cling film sandwich rings attach magnetically, making the assembly fast and reproducible.

**Positional accuracy**
The insert slots into the stage in a fixed orientation, providing high repeat positioning accuracy between imaging sessions.

|-----------|-------|
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |

## Assembly

1. Press-fit magnets into the four recesses in the main body — use a small amount of cyanoacrylate adhesive if fit is loose
2. Cut cling film to size and place between the two sandwich rings
3. Attach the sandwich ring assembly to the main body via the magnets
4. Fix the cling film on the objective side using the objective heater heating element as a clamp
5. Place the inverted 1 mL Petri dish onto the insert and position under the objective
